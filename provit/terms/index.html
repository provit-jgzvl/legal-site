2. The service

Provit is a local beauty-clip studio for iOS. You can:

  • Record vertical beauty clips with camera and microphone, or import videos from Photos
  • Preview clips, add a title and category, and save them on this device
  • Publish clips to a Demo Studio feed that lives entirely on this device
  • Optionally spend Coins on a Demo Boost that only changes local sort order for 24 hours
  • Use local tools: Look Mood Journal, Beauty Prep Checklist, and Look Goals
  • Buy Coins through Apple In-App Purchase

Provit does not operate a live social network, server-backed community, or real content distribution. Scripted personas, seed clips, tips, and boost placement are demos and carry a Demo badge. A Demo Boost does not deliver real exposure, followers, or income.

3. Accounts

  • Quick Login creates random local credentials stored in the device Keychain. No remote account is created.
  • Logout ends the local session and returns you to Login. Credentials remain so you can sign in again. Clips and Coins are not deleted by Logout.
  • After Logout, a cold start always opens Login. The App does not auto-enter Studio.
  • Delete Account permanently wipes local credentials, session, projects, media, demo history, tools data, Coin balance, and purchase records on this device. Coins are consumable and cannot be restored after deletion.

4. Virtual currency and In-App Purchases

  • Coins are consumable virtual credits sold only through Apple’s StoreKit / App Store.
  • Payment is processed by Apple. Provit never collects or stores payment card details.
  • Coin packs are listed by price ascending. Purchasability and displayed price come from the App Store after a product query.
  • Coins stay on this device. They are not transferable and cannot be restored after you delete the App, delete your account, or clear local data.
  • Demo Boost costs 10 Coins and lasts 24 hours. Preview never spends Coins; only an explicit Confirm Boost does.
  • Apple’s standard refund and purchase policies apply. Provit does not offer a Restore Purchases feature for consumable Coins.

5. Acceptable use and community standards (UGC)

Provit includes user-generated clip titles and a demo feed. There is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour, including sexual or suggestive content, harassment, threats, hate speech, illegal goods or services, promotion of self-harm, and impersonation.

  • Titles are filtered before publish (including light normalization of look-alike characters).
  • You may Report a clip from Clip Detail or by long-pressing a feed row. Reporting hides the clip immediately, ejects the author from your local session, creates a local ticket ID, and opens your email client so you can send the report to the developer.
  • You may Block an author (two-step confirm) and Hide a row with a swipe action.
  • The developer aims to review and act on reports within 24 hours.
