2. The service

Provit is a local beauty short-video studio for iOS. You can:

  • Swipe a full-screen vertical Studio feed of seed clips and clips you publish, all stored on this device
  • Open a creator profile from a clip, Follow creators, and Chat locally with clip publishers (replies are scripted on this device)
  • Browse Posts — image-and-text makeup-tool guides (browse and like only)
  • Record vertical beauty clips with camera and microphone, or import videos from Photos
  • Preview clips, add a title and category, and publish them to the on-device Studio feed
  • Spend Coins on a Boost from the lightning control next to a Studio clip (labelled 10), from More, from Me, or from Coin Store. A Boost recommends any Studio clip — yours or another creator’s — first in your local feed for 24 hours
  • Use local tools from Me → Look tools: Look Mood Journal, Beauty Prep Checklist, and Look Goals
  • Buy Coins through Apple In-App Purchase

Provit does not operate a live social network, server-backed community, or real content distribution. Seed personas, seed clips, tool posts, and chat replies are scripted on-device content. A Boost does not deliver real exposure, followers, or income. Nothing about a Boost is sent off this device.

3. Accounts

  • Quick Login creates random local credentials stored in the device Keychain. No remote account is created. The first Quick Login on an account grants a one-time welcome balance equal to twice the $0.99 Coin pack (Faint Glimmer: 100 Coins × 2 = 200 Coins). Logout does not grant that welcome again. Delete Account resets it so a new Quick Login can grant it once more.
  • Logout ends the local session and returns you to Login. Credentials remain so you can sign in again. Clips and Coins are not deleted by Logout.
  • After Logout, a cold start always opens Login. The App does not auto-enter Studio.
  • Delete Account (Me → Account) permanently wipes local credentials, session, projects, media, Studio history, chats, follows, tools data, Coin balance, purchase records, and the welcome-grant flag on this device. Coins are consumable and cannot be restored after deletion.
  • There is no “Clear All Local Data” control in Settings. Content wipe is Delete Account. Uninstalling the App also removes the App container according to iOS behaviour.

4. Virtual currency and In-App Purchases

  • Coins are consumable virtual credits. Paid packs are sold only through Apple’s StoreKit / App Store. A one-time welcome grant of twice the $0.99 pack is added on first Quick Login and is not an App Store purchase.
  • Payment is processed by Apple. Provit never collects or stores payment card details.
  • Coin packs are listed by price ascending. Purchasability and displayed price come from the App Store after a product query. Product identifiers are never shown in the UI.
  • Coins stay on this device. They are not transferable and cannot be restored after you delete the App or delete your account.
  • Boost is the only spend. It costs 10 Coins and lasts 24 hours. You can Boost your own clips or another creator’s clips from Studio. Opening Boost Review does not charge; only Confirm Boost does. Preview, publish, chat, follow, and Posts likes never spend Coins.
  • Apple’s standard refund and purchase policies apply. Provit does not offer a Restore Purchases feature for consumable Coins.

5. Acceptable use and community standards (UGC)

Provit includes user-generated clip titles, a Studio feed, Posts likes, and local chat. There is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour, including explicit or suggestive material, harassment, threats, hate speech, illegal goods or services, promotion of self-harm, and impersonation.

  • Titles and chat messages are filtered before save (including a blocklist and light normalization of look-alike characters).
  • You may Report a clip or a creator from Studio More (scope chooser). Reporting updates local visibility immediately, creates a local ticket ID, copies it, and opens your email client so you can send the report to the developer.
  • You may Block a clip or a creator from Studio More (scope chooser; creator block is two-step). Blocking a creator hides their clips and posts and ends chat with them on this device.

  • The developer aims to review and act on reports within 24 hours by removing content and ejecting the offending user from the local session as implemented in the App.
